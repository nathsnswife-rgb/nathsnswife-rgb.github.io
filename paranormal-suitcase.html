NO PARANORMAL MAGIC CLAIMS

Your phone is a sensor platform.
We tell you what it is actually doing.

01

Real hardware

Tools use the microphone, camera, magnetometer, accelerometer, compass and supported barometer hardware that is actually present on the device.

02

Clear explanations

Every instrument includes a ? information panel explaining the signal being measured or processed and the limitations that matter.

03

You interpret the result

INSIDE THE SUITCASE

A whole field kit.
One phone.

Two core tools are free. The rest of the investigation suite is available with the Premium unlock.

FREE🎙

EVP Recorder

Capture raw microphone audio, place event markers during a session and scrub back through saved recordings.

FREE▥

K2 / EMF

Monitor real magnetometer change from a local baseline with five K2-style indicator levels.

PREMIUM◉

EVP Enhance

Create a separate filtered and amplified copy while preserving the original raw recording untouched.

PREMIUM📻

Spirit Box

Generated static with optional short sweeps of live internet-radio audio, adjustable sweep behavior and source logging.

PREMIUM⌁

SLS Camera

Visible-light pose mapping using on-device pose detection. It is not infrared, depth sensing or Kinect hardware.

PREMIUM◎

REM Pod

Combines optical disturbance, magnetometer change and motion rejection to watch the area immediately around the phone.

PREMIUM≋

Geophone

Uses the accelerometer to detect vibration and movement through the phone or the surface it is resting on.

PREMIUM↕

Pressure Monitor

Tracks atmospheric pressure on phones with a barometer. If the hardware is not there, the app does not invent a reading.

PREMIUM⌖

Anomaly Compass

Records magnetic-field changes by direction during a deliberate 360° sweep for comparison and documentation.

PREMIUM◫

Motion Camera

Compares visible camera frames and can capture a still image when enough of the scene changes.

PREMIUM▣

Evidence Camera

Capture investigation photographs, including an optional sensor-overlay camera for timestamped context.

PREMIUM●

Room Watch

Run an unattended session with audio recording and environmental event markers, then review everything in the journal.
