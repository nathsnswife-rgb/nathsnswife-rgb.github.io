EQUIPMENT FIELD GUIDE

KNOW YOUR TOOLS.

A closer look at what the equipment does, how to use it, and what the readings actually mean. Hardware support and availability vary by device and platform.

01 / MAGNETIC FIELD

EMF Style Detector

Monitor the phone magnetometer’s measured field in microtesla (µT), compare against a calibrated baseline and watch the five-light meter respond to changes. Useful for documenting environmental variations; not a detector of spirits.

02 / ORIGINAL AUDIO

EVP Recorder

Capture microphone audio as 48 kHz, 16-bit WAV, observe the live waveform, pause recording, mark moments of interest and replay the unaltered original. Keep an auditable record of what you actually heard.

03 / AUDIO REVIEW

EVP Enhance

Record original audio, then compare raw playback with an enhanced copy. Adjustable enhancement can help you examine quiet details while preserving the untouched original for comparison. Processing can introduce artifacts, so always check both versions.

04 / AUDIO EXPERIMENT

Spirit Box

Explore a sweeping, radio-inspired audio interface with adjustable static, bleed and sweep speed. Start a session and mark moments worth revisiting. The visual frequency display is not a claim that the phone contains a hardware AM/FM tuner.

05 / PROXIMITY EXPERIMENT

REM Pod Style

A five-light REM Pod-inspired interface with arming, calibration, sensitivity and mute controls. It uses available phone capabilities rather than claiming to be a standalone REM Pod or to measure a spirit directly.

06 / VIBRATION

Geophone

Place the phone on a stable surface and use its motion sensors to observe taps, footsteps, knocks and vibrations transmitted through that surface. See live movement, peaks, averages, duration and marked events.

07 / MAGNETIC SWEEP

Anomaly Compass

Perform a deliberate 360-degree sweep while tracking the phone’s heading and magnetic-field variation. Compare readings by direction; the compass does not point toward paranormal sources.

08 / CAMERA ANALYSIS

Shadow Scanner

Calibrate a stationary camera scene, adjust sensitivity, lock the view, watch for image changes, mark events and record clips. Lighting shifts, movement and camera noise can also produce detections.

09 / UNATTENDED MONITORING

Room Watch

Name a room, choose an exit timer and configure audio capture. Leave the phone monitoring the space while compatible sensors automatically mark events in the investigation journal. Choose raw audio, enhanced comparison or no continuous audio.

10 / INTERACTIVE FIELD TOOL

Dead Bell

A touch-activated bell with configurable sound-level and vibration sensitivity, bell response mode and a dedicated event log. Its interface separates user interactions from monitored responses.

11 / INVESTIGATION ARCHIVE

Case Files

Organize investigations, sessions and saved evidence in a searchable archive. Revisit what happened, where it happened and which recordings or observations deserve a second look.

12 / FIELD WORKFLOW

Start Investigation

Name an investigation, optionally save its location and begin with available tools or customize your equipment first. Keep your fieldwork tied together as one investigation rather than scattered across unrelated screens.

13 / FIELD UTILITY

Flashlight

Use the phone’s light with standard illumination, fast strobe and SOS modes when supported. A practical utility for working in dark locations; strobe effects should be used responsibly.
