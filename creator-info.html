CREATORS · PODCASTS · INVESTIGATORS

Tell strange stories?

Good. We like those people.

Roadside Strange works naturally with paranormal investigators, folklore researchers, cryptid creators, UAP channels, history storytellers, podcasters and road-trip creators.

Complimentary Premium

Selected creators may receive complimentary Premium so they can use the full app before deciding whether it is relevant to their work or audience.

No scripted reviews

There is no expectation of a positive review and no obligation to post. Complimentary access does not buy an opinion.

Natural collaborations

Episode-linked cases, eyewitness material, themed collections, creator recommendations and strange-road-trip ideas can all make sense when there is a genuine fit.

Creator independence

Keep your own voice. Roadside Strange is more interested in useful long-term relationships than promotional copy nobody believes.

What Roadside Strange covers

Hauntings, ghost lore, cryptid reports, UAP cases, strange history, local legends and other location-tied stories that make people want to know what happened there.

How the case library is handled

Cases are researched from material such as public records, historical sources, news archives, official reports, local history and folklore, location-specific sources and eyewitness material where available. Cases are reviewed and rewritten for Roadside Strange rather than automatically generated and published. Community submissions are reviewed before they become public.
