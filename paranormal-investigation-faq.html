Can a smartphone really detect EMF?

Compatible smartphones contain magnetometers capable of measuring magnetic fields. Paranormal Suitcase uses these readings for magnetic-field monitoring. Smartphone magnetometers have different capabilities and limitations from dedicated professional EMF equipment.
