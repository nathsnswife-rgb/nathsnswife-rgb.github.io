Paranormal Suitcase

Help with investigation tools, recordings, permissions and your optional lifetime premium unlock.

Lifetime Premium & Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. On iPhone, open the app's Settings and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that originally purchased it.

For Apple refunds or payment issues, use Report a Problem.

Recordings & Evidence

Review recordings, sessions, photos and event markers in the app's evidence area. If recording fails, check microphone permissions and try a short test. Back up important originals before reinstalling.

Cameras & Sensors

Check each tool's information button for what it measures and its limitations. Enable camera or microphone permissions where required. When reporting an issue, include your device model, software version and a screenshot.

Privacy & Accuracy

Paranormal Suitcase is an investigation and documentation toolkit. Sensor readings and camera analysis do not independently prove paranormal activity. Read the Paranormal Suitcase Privacy Policy.

Roadside Strange

Help with app problems, community reports, map corrections, privacy requests and lifetime access.

App Problems & Bugs

Use in-app support or reporting when available. Tell us what you tapped, what you expected, what happened, your device and app version. Screenshots are especially helpful.

Lifetime Unlock

Roadside Strange offers a one-time lifetime unlock, not a recurring subscription. If you need help with a purchase or access, contact us and include the store and account used for the purchase—never your password.

Community Content

Use the app's reporting tools for community content that may violate the rules. Reports are reviewed rather than automatically published.

Map Corrections

Is a location closed, moved, inaccessible or routed incorrectly? Include the case name and any safe, accurate location details so we can review it.

Privacy & Account Deletion

Use the in-app Privacy / Legal Request option for data access, correction or deletion. Contributor accounts can request deletion in the app or through the account deletion page.

Safety & Location Access

Roadside Strange is an exploration guide, not permission to enter property. Respect signs, closures, boundaries and local regulations. Never trespass or enter unsafe locations.
