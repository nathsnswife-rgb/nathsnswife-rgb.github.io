Four places. Four unsettling stories. A new collection of mysteries to discover each week.

CASE 01
Adams, Tennessee

THE BELL WITCH

In the early 1800s, the Bell family reported strange knocks, voices, and attacks at their Tennessee farm. The legend of the Bell Witch remains one of America’s best-known haunting tales.

CASE 02
Petros, Tennessee

BRUSHY MOUNTAIN

Once a notorious maximum-security prison, Brushy Mountain housed dangerous inmates for more than a century. Visitors and former staff have shared eerie stories of footsteps, voices, and lingering presences.

CASE 03
Nashville, Tennessee

RYMAN AUDITORIUM

Known as the Mother Church of Country Music, the Ryman has hosted generations of performers. Its ghost stories include sightings of a mysterious figure called the Gray Man in the auditorium.

CASE 04
South Pittsburg, Tennessee

OLD SOUTH PITTSBURG HOSPITAL

This former hospital has become a destination for paranormal investigators, with reports of unexplained sounds, shadowy figures, and strange encounters in its darkened rooms and corridors.

Atmospheric artwork is illustrative, not documentary photography. Haunting accounts are folklore and reported experiences.

BUCK & BISCUIT

Two friends. Zero sense of direction. Endless adventures.

Meet Buck and Biscuit: two unlikely adventurers with wildly different ideas about what constitutes a good time.

Buck is a cowboy with a thirst for adventure, a talent for finding trouble, and absolutely no concept of personal safety. If there's a dangerous shortcut, a suspicious noise, or something that really shouldn't be touched, Buck's already halfway there.

Biscuit, meanwhile, is a four-legged chicken with a functioning brain. He's scared of just about everything, but somehow manages to be the sensible one. His days are spent questioning Buck's decisions, avoiding certain death, and wondering why he couldn't have been assigned a nice, quiet field.

Together, they're proof that bravery and common sense rarely travel in the same saddle.

Meet Angel & Nathan

The people behind the apps, the adventures, and the unexplained.

Two people. A thousand questions. An open road.

We love the strange stories hiding in ordinary places.

We're Angel and Nathan, a husband-and-wife team living in Arizona with our two children and a household full of animals, including cats, axolotls, reptiles, and turtles.

We're explorers, creators, paranormal enthusiasts, and the sort of people who see an unfamiliar road and immediately wonder what's at the other end. Our lives have never been particularly ordinary. And we wouldn't have it any other way.

OUR STORY

A shared love of the unknown

Angel's fascination with the paranormal began when she was very young. Long before smartphone investigation tools, she was asking questions about ghosts, unexplained experiences, and the mysteries that still don't have easy answers.

Nathan had always been fascinated by ghosts, too, but Angel introduced him to paranormal investigating shortly before they married. Exploring the unexplained has been part of their life together ever since.

They even spent their first wedding anniversary in Virginia City, Nevada, a historic mining town famous for its ghost stories.

BEYOND THE INVESTIGATIONS

Adventure is part of the deal

When we're not investigating, there's a good chance we're planning our next adventure. We love gold prospecting, camping, exploring old roads, and discovering forgotten places. Some of our favorite days are spent heading out into the Arizona desert to see what's around the next bend.

Photography, art, and creating things from scratch are among Angel's passions. Nathan plays guitar and loves classic cars, including his 1968 Dodge Coronet.

Between our family, our animals, and our latest projects, there's rarely a quiet moment in our household. Somehow, we always find another project.

How our adventures became apps

Why we built Roadside Strange

We were always looking for unusual places to visit, but the information was scattered everywhere. Haunted locations on one site. Cryptid sightings on another. UFO reports and strange history buried somewhere else.

We wanted to discover them all in one place. Eventually, we stopped wishing someone would make that app and built Roadside Strange ourselves.

Why we built Paranormal Suitcase

We love paranormal investigating, but we don't have thousands of dollars to spend on specialist equipment. We knew we couldn't be the only ones.

So we explored what modern phones can genuinely do with their microphones, cameras, and available sensors. Paranormal Suitcase brings investigation-style tools together in your pocket—without pretending a phone replaces every piece of professional equipment.

OUR INTEGRITY PROMISE

Real observations. Your interpretation. No fabricated evidence.

We don't manufacture paranormal activity to make an investigation more exciting. We don't want an app that invents ghost messages, produces fake encounters, or deliberately convinces someone they've captured evidence when they haven't.

Magnetic-field changes aren't automatically ghosts. Unusual sounds aren't necessarily voices. Strange shapes in photographs aren't proof of hauntings. But they can still be worth investigating.

We would rather spend three hours investigating and find absolutely nothing than spend ten minutes collecting evidence we know is fake.

We won't promise you ghosts.
We promise we won't invent them for you.

More than just two apps

Desert Nights Studio LLC brings together the things we love: paranormal investigation, gold prospecting, road trips, photography, storytelling, technology, and creativity.

We're not a faceless development company. We're a husband-and-wife team raising a family, exploring the world around us, and turning our experiences into projects we hope other people will enjoy.

We build things we'd want to use ourselves. And if our apps encourage someone to take a different road, discover an unusual place, or start asking questions, we've accomplished something worthwhile.

Sometimes the best stories aren't the ones you're told.
They're the ones you go out and discover.
