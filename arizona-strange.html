Haunted places and strange stories across Arizona

Arizona is packed with the kind of places Roadside Strange was built for: lonely desert roads, historic mines, old bridges, mountain forests and skies tied to some of the state's best-known unexplained stories. This featured-state collection brings together real locations, regional legends and documented reports so you can discover the story before deciding whether to make the stop yourself.

For paranormal travelers, Arizona offers everything from reported hauntings and ghost stories to cryptid legends and famous UFO and UAP cases. Roadside Strange keeps those categories connected to actual places and safe public access whenever possible, rather than treating them as disconnected stories floating around the internet.

Arizona hauntings, cryptids and UFO cases

Start with Gillespie Dam Bridge for an eerie historic crossing with reported paranormal activity, or Vulture Mine for a mix of mining history, desert atmosphere and ghost stories. Head north into the high country for the Mogollon Monster, Arizona's best-known cryptid legend, or look to the sky with the Phoenix Lights, one of the most famous mass UFO and UAP sightings associated with the state.

This page is part of Roadside Strange's rotating featured-state series. New states and new case pages will be added regularly, giving searchers and road-trippers a growing collection of haunted places, cryptid reports, UAP sightings and weird history to explore.
