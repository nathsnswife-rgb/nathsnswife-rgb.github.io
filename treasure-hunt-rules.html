Mystery Treasure Hunt • November 2026

The $100 Mystery Treasure Hunt

$100 USD GRAND PRIZE

One world of strange places. Hidden clues. One final mystery. Explore Roadside Strange and get ready to solve it.

Prelaunch notice — entries are not open.

Scheduled start: November 15, 2026, at 12:00 noon Arizona time (MST). Planned end: December 15, 2026, at 11:59:59 PM MST. These rules may be updated before entries open. The entry form, clues, and final rules will be published here before the contest begins.

1. Sponsor and contact

This competition is organized by Nathan Wood, an individual operating Roadside Strange in Arizona, USA. For competition questions contact nathan@roadsidestrange.com. No in-person visits or physical submissions are accepted.

2. Who may enter

Open to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are 18 or older at the time of entry, where permitted by law. Void where prohibited. The organizer, puzzle creators, anyone given advance access to the answer, and members of their households cannot enter.

3. Free entry

No purchase necessary. No Premium subscription required. The Roadside Strange app is free to download and is the intended way to discover the locations. All information necessary to participate will also be accessible without purchasing or subscribing. A free-access alternative will be provided if an app download is unavailable. No paid feature will confer an advantage.

4. How the hunt works

Use the published clues to investigate Roadside Strange locations and publicly accessible Google Street View imagery. Follow the instructions to find details and letters, then solve the final mystery word or phrase. No physical travel, trespassing, or entry onto private property is necessary. The organizer will verify clues and document the intended solution before launch.

5. Entering

Once entries open, submit your final answer through the official form linked from this page. One entry per person. The form will request a name, email address, and answer. Social media messages, emails, and phone calls are not entries. Submissions must be received before the published deadline. Do not send an answer yet.

6. Winner selection

This is a skill-based puzzle contest, not a random drawing. After entries close, the organizer will verify the earliest correctly answered entry received from an eligible participant. Server-recorded receipt time will determine order; if timestamps are identical, the submission system's unique sequential receipt order will break the tie. The answer and judging criteria will be fixed before launch. If there is no eligible correct entry, no prize will be awarded.

7. Prize and notification

One eligible winner receives $100 USD. No fee is required to claim the prize. The provisional winner will be contacted by email within seven days after the contest closes, and must respond within seven days. If the provisional winner cannot be verified or does not respond, the next earliest eligible correct entry may be considered. The prize will be sent using an available lawful payment method within 30 days of verification. The winner is responsible for any applicable taxes.

8. Fair play

Public research and collaboration are allowed. Multiple entries, false identities, automated mass submissions, interference with the entry system, and tampering with timestamps are prohibited. Any disqualification must be supported by documented evidence and applied consistently.

9. Clue corrections and outages

If a clue is materially wrong or unavailable, the organizer will publish a correction and make a fair adjustment if necessary. Significant entry-system outages may result in a publicly announced deadline extension. The organizer will not secretly change the solution or disadvantage eligible participants.

10. Privacy

Entry information will be used only to administer the competition, prevent fraud, verify eligibility, contact the winner, and deliver the prize. Entering does not enroll anyone in marketing. Only necessary information will be collected, retained for a limited period needed for administration and legal compliance, and handled under the Roadside Strange privacy policy and a contest-specific notice provided with the entry form. Winner photographs or testimonials will not be used without separate permission unless required by law.

11. Third-party services

This competition is independent of Google, Apple, and Google Play, and is not sponsored, endorsed, or administered by them. Google Maps and Street View are third-party services subject to their own terms. Imagery and availability may change.

12. General conditions

The organizer may address fraud, technical problems, or legal requirements through publicly announced, fair adjustments. Applicable federal and state laws govern. Nothing in these rules waives rights that cannot legally be waived.

Before launch: The entry form, puzzle, technical judging process, final privacy notice, and any necessary rule corrections must be completed and tested. This page does not accept entries.
