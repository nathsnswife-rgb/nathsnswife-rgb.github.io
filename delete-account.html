Delete your account

How to request account deletion and what happens next.

Service: Roadside Strange by DivineApps

You can request deletion of a Roadside Strange contributor account from inside the app. If you cannot access the app, use the public request route below.

Request account deletion ↗Read the Privacy Policy

What deletion covers

After a verified deletion request is completed, the contributor account and associated personal information are deleted or anonymized as applicable. This can include authentication records and private contributor information linked to the account.

Community content

Comments or approved community material may be removed, anonymized or retained depending on the request, moderation history, fraud or security needs, legal obligations, or the need to establish or defend legal claims.

What may be retained

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for fraud and security prevention, legal compliance, resolving disputes, or establishing or defending legal claims.

Other privacy requests

Access, correction and other privacy requests can be submitted through the Privacy / Legal Request option inside Roadside Strange. See the Privacy Policy for more information.
