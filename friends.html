Friend of Roadside Strange · Paranormal radio & podcast

The Angel Rock with Lorilei Potvin

Meet our friend Lorilei Potvin, the Canadian paranormal enthusiast and host of The Angel Rock on the United Public Radio Network. Drawing on her own experiences living in a reportedly haunted home, Lorilei explores hauntings, spirit communication, UFOs, cryptids and other unexplained mysteries. She also shared her story on Travel Channel’s Paranormal Survivor. We’re happy to support a fellow creator who loves connecting people through their stories.
